Workflow

How The Diagnostic Pipeline Works

A streamlined end-to-end computer vision workflow translating raw foliar images into biological diagnoses.

Step 1
Upload Foliar Photo

Capture or upload a clear, focused image of the affected plant leaf. Formats like JPG, PNG, and WEBP are verified and sanitized.

Step 2
CNN Deep Inference

The leaf is preprocessed to 224×224 and evaluated by MobileNetV2 depthwise separable convolution layers to compute disease probability distributions.

Step 3
Diagnosis & Care Advice

Receive instant condition diagnosis, prediction confidence gauge, top-3 candidates, symptoms breakdown, and agricultural treatment recommendations.
